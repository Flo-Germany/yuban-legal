Yuban

Android application for learning Chinese in conversation. The application is currently in a closed test.

The application

Yuban holds conversations in Chinese. Replies appear in Chinese characters; any word can be tapped to show its pinyin, meaning and every reading from the bundled dictionary. In addition, the application arranges sentences by structure and explains recognised grammar patterns.

Vocabulary trainer

Saved words are reviewed in short units. Each unit tests character, pinyin and meaning, adds listening and writing exercises and closes with a review of the words that are not yet secure.

Notes

The application is in a closed test. Features may change, and errors are possible during this phase.
