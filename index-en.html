Learn Chinese in conversation.

Yuban is your Mandarin language partner: real conversations instead of word lists, every word explained on tap, plus a vocabulary trainer with tones, listening and writing.

  • Conversations, not word lists You answer in Chinese. Every character can be tapped: pinyin, meaning and all readings come from the dictionary.
  • Words that stick Short six-word lessons with character, pinyin and meaning, tones, dictation and free writing.
  • Listen and speak Device voice for every word, listening practice with short stories and speaking drills with recognition.
