Closed test

Yuban is an Android application for learning Chinese in conversation. A small group is sought for the closed test, accompanying the application over a period of 14 days.

What the test provides

  • Early access to Yuban on Android.
  • The complete vocabulary trainer, including tone and listening exercises.
  • Direct contact with the developer.

Requirement

  • The application remains installed until the end of the test period.

Correct the address

Registration

An email address is sufficient. Testers are added in small groups, which may take a few days.

Registration saved

The registration has been saved. An email follows once a decision has been made about admission to the test – also in the event of a rejection.
